# Biometric Attendance System: How It Works and How to Choose One

A biometric attendance system records when employees arrive and leave by checking a physical trait, usually a fingerprint or face, instead of a card or signature. Because the trait belongs to one person, it stops colleagues clocking in for each other ("buddy punching"). The data feeds payroll, so overtime and absences are calculated from real records.

## How it works

1. **Enrolment.** The employee's fingerprint or face is scanned once. The device converts it into a template, a mathematical record, rather than storing a photo. `[CONFIRM how TimeTech devices store templates]`
2. **Clocking.** At the device, the employee scans again. The device compares the scan with the stored templates.
3. **Logging.** If it matches, the device records the employee, date and time.
4. **Syncing.** The log passes to the attendance software by network or cloud.
5. **Payroll.** The software applies shift rules and sends hours, late arrivals and overtime to payroll.

## Types of biometric attendance

| Method | Strengths | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fingerprint | Mature, low cost, fast | Worn or dry fingers can fail; some people dislike touch devices |
| Face recognition | Contactless, quick, works for large groups | Needs good lighting and a quality camera; privacy questions |
| Palm or vein | High accuracy | Higher cost, less common |
| Iris | Very accurate | Expensive; usually for high-security areas |
| Card plus PIN (not biometric) | Cheap, simple | Cards can be shared, so buddy punching returns |

Many sites choose fingerprint for general staff and face recognition where hygiene or speed matters, such as hospitals and factories. See our comparison of [fingerprint attendance systems](/blog/fingerprint-attendance-system).

## What to compare

- **Accuracy and speed.** Ask for a trial with your own staff, including those who work with their hands.
- **Capacity.** The number of employees and logs the device stores, and what happens if the network is down.
- **Software.** Shift rules, overtime, leave, reports and an Arabic interface.
- **Integration.** Does attendance data flow into payroll automatically?
- **Remote and mobile staff.** Mobile or geo-fenced clocking for field teams.
- **Environment.** Dust, heat and outdoor use need suitable hardware.
- **Support.** Local installation, warranty and spare devices.

## Privacy and employee trust

Fingerprints and faces are sensitive personal data. Tell employees what you collect, why, who can see it and how long you keep it. Bahrain and Saudi Arabia both have personal data protection laws that apply. `[VERIFY and link the official texts]` Choose a system that stores templates rather than raw images and lets you delete leavers' data.

## Connecting attendance to payroll

Manual copying from clock reports into payroll is where many errors start. When the attendance software sits in the same system as payroll, late minutes, absences and overtime flow straight into the payslip. This is the main reason many employers choose an integrated attendance and HR system over standalone devices.

## A simple selection process

1. Count employees, sites and shifts.
2. Decide which methods suit each site.
3. Ask for a pilot on one site for two weeks.
4. Compare error rates, speed and complaints.
5. Check payroll integration with a real pay run.

## FAQ

**What is a biometric attendance system?**
A system that records attendance by verifying a person's fingerprint, face or other physical trait.

**Is face recognition better than fingerprint?**
It depends on the site. Face recognition is contactless and fast; fingerprint is cheaper and proven. Many companies use both.

**Can biometric attendance be faked?**
It is much harder than sharing a card. Good devices include liveness checks for faces. `[CONFIRM]`

**Does it work when the internet is down?**
Most devices store logs locally and sync later. Check the storage capacity.

**Is biometric attendance legal in Bahrain?**
It is widely used, but data protection rules apply. Inform employees and protect the data. `[VERIFY]`
