# Salary Slip Format in Bahrain: Free Template and What to Include

A salary slip (also called a payslip) is the document that shows an employee how their monthly pay was calculated: what they earned, what was deducted and what reached their bank account. This page gives you the fields to include, a worked example in Bahraini dinars (BHD) and a template you can copy.

> Note: a salary slip is different from a salary certificate. A salary certificate is a letter confirming an employee's salary, often requested for banks or visas. This page covers the monthly salary slip.

## What a salary slip should show

Whether or not a specific field is required by law, a clear slip prevents disputes. `[VERIFY what Bahrain law and LMRA/Ministry guidance require on pay records]`

**Employer details**
- Company name and commercial registration number
- Address or logo

**Employee details**
- Full name
- Employee number
- Job title and department
- CPR or ID number (consider showing only the last digits for privacy)
- Bank account (partial)

**Pay period**
- Month and year
- Payment date and method (bank transfer)

**Earnings**
- Basic salary
- Allowances (housing, transport, other)
- Overtime
- Bonus or commission

**Deductions**
- Social insurance contribution, where applicable `[VERIFY rates and who pays by nationality]`
- Unpaid leave or absence
- Loan or advance repayments
- Other agreed deductions

**Totals**
- Gross pay
- Total deductions
- Net pay

## Salary slip and the wage protection system

Bahrain runs a wage protection system under which private-sector salaries are paid through banks and reported electronically. `[VERIFY current name, scope and rules]` Your slip should match the amount paid in the bank file. If the slip says 732.000 and the bank file says 700.000, you will have a dispute. Prepare the payroll once and produce both from the same data.

## Common mistakes

1. **Slip does not match the bank transfer.** Always generate both from the same payroll run.
2. **Missing overtime or allowance lines.** Employees cannot check what they cannot see.
3. **Unexplained deductions.** Label each deduction clearly.
4. **Manual errors.** Typing from attendance sheets into a spreadsheet is the usual cause. Connect attendance to payroll to avoid it.
5. **Sharing slips insecurely.** Send them through a secure portal, not a group chat.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between a salary slip and a payslip?**
Nothing in practice. Both mean the monthly statement of pay. "Salary slip" is more common in the Gulf and India; "payslip" in the UK.

**Is a salary slip mandatory in Bahrain?**
Employers should keep clear pay records and give employees a statement of pay as good practice. Check the current legal requirement with the Ministry of Labour or your legal adviser. `[VERIFY]`

**Can I make a salary slip in Excel?**
Yes. Use the template above, and keep a locked copy of each month's slip.

**What should be deducted from a salary slip in Bahrain?**
Only deductions allowed by law or agreed in writing, such as social insurance, unpaid leave and agreed loan repayments. `[VERIFY]`

**What is the difference between a salary slip and a salary certificate?**
A salary slip is a monthly breakdown. A salary certificate is a letter confirming current salary, often needed by banks.

**Can employees get their salary slip online?**
Yes. An HRMS with an employee portal lets staff view and download slips at any time.
